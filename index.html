About the Studio

Where quiet luxury meets flawless craftsmanship.

Lumière NYC was founded on a single belief: your nails deserve the same care as a bespoke garment. Our technicians train in the Russian dry manicure method — a slow, precise technique that respects the natural nail.

From clean girl minimalism to intricate custom art, every visit is a private, unhurried ritual designed around you.
